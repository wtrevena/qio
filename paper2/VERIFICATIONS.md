## 1. The SU(2)_L obstruction is now a machine-checked lemma (NOTES item 6)

**Claim verified.** The group of unitaries on C^8 that map the mode span
F = span_C{f_1, f_2, f_3} into itself under conjugation is exactly
{e^{i phi} G(U) : U in U(3)} — the number-conserving (mode) gauge group is U(3)
times a global phase, and no SU(2)_L can act as a gauge symmetry on this
one-generation ideal.

**Lemma (mode gauge group of three JW modes; machine-checked).**
Let f_1 = sm(x)I(x)I, f_2 = Z(x)sm(x)I, f_3 = Z(x)Z(x)sm on C^8 and
F = span_C{f_1, f_2, f_3} in M_8(C). Then:

(i) *(Lie algebra.)* L := {h = h^dag : [h, f_k] in F, k = 1, 2, 3} has
    **dim_R L = 10 = dim u(3) + 1**, and L = dGamma(u(3)) (+) R·1, where
    dGamma(a) = Sum_ij a_ij f_i^dag f_j. Every element of L commutes with the
    number operator N.
(ii) *(Exponentiation.)* For every h in L, W = e^{ih} satisfies
    W f_k W^dag = Sum_j M_kj f_j with M in U(3), and W = e^{i phi} G(M^dag)
    exactly.
(iii) *(Converse — group level, all components.)* Any unitary W with
    W F W^dag <= F is of the form e^{i phi} G(U): the CAR force the coefficient
    matrix M to satisfy M M^dag = 1 (from {W f_i W^dag, (W f_j W^dag)^dag} =
    delta_ij), and W G(M^dag)^dag commutes with every f_k, f_k^dag, which
    generate all of M_8(C), so it is scalar by Schur. No extra connected or
    disconnected components exist.
(iv) *(The SU(2)_L obstruction proper.)* The centralizer of the colour action
    {G(V) : V in SU(3)} inside L is span_R{1, N}: **2-dimensional and abelian**.
    An SU(2)_L gauge factor must commute with SU(3)_c and act on the modes;
    su(2) needs a 3-dimensional non-abelian algebra. There is none.

**Method.** The constraint [h, f_k] in F for k = 1,2,3 is linear in the 64 real
parameters of a Hermitian 8x8 matrix; it was assembled as a real 384x64 matrix
(HS-orthogonal complement of F applied to each commutator, real and imaginary
parts stacked) and its nullspace computed by SVD. Exponentiation, M-extraction
(M_kj = Tr(f_j^dag W f_k W^dag)/4), unitarity, and phase-times-G(U)
reconstruction were checked for all 10 basis elements and 20 random real
combinations. The algebra generated by {f_k, f_k^dag} was grown to closure. The
SU(3)-centralizer was computed as a second nullspace over 20 Haar SU(3) samples
and certified on 20 fresh samples.

**Result (exact numbers).**
* dim_R L = **10**; SVD split is unambiguous: largest "zero" singular value
  1.13e-15 vs smallest nonzero singular value 1.000.
* Constraint residual of the solution basis 9.3e-16; ||[h, N]|| <= 1.1e-15
  (every solution is number-conserving); span check L = dGamma(u(3)) (+) R·1
  exact (rank 10 = 10).
* Exponentiation (30 elements): max ||W f_k W^dag - Sum_j M_kj f_j|| = 2.8e-15;
  max ||M M^dag - 1|| = 4.7e-16; max ||W - e^{i phi} G(M^dag)|| = **2.5e-15**.
* Algebra generated by {f_k, f_k^dag}: dim **64** = M_8(C) (Schur step valid).
* SU(3)-centralizer in L: dim **2**, equals span{1, N} (rank check exact),
  fresh-sample commutation residual 1.1e-15, basis commutator < 1e-12 (abelian).

**Bogoliubov / particle-hole / antilinear variants (also machine-checked).**
Allowing f_i -> Sum_j (U_ij f_j + V_ij f_j^dag), i.e. the constraint
[h, f_k] in F (+) F^dag, enlarges the solution space to **dim_R = 16 =
dim so(6) + 1** (identified exactly as dGamma(u(3)) (+) R·1 (+) the six pair
terms f_i^dag f_j^dag + h.c., i(f_i^dag f_j^dag − h.c.); rank checks exact).
But the weight grading kills all of it:

* {h in L_B : [h, N] = 0} = L exactly (dim 10, span identical) — every added
  direction violates particle number;
* the added directions do not preserve F: for the pair generator
  f_1^dag f_2^dag + h.c., ||[h, N]|| = 4.000, the component of [h, f_1]
  orthogonal to F has norm 2.000 (it is −2 f_2^dag, entirely outside F), and
  e^{ih} has off-weight-block norm 0.841 (it moves states across charge
  sectors); a random element of L_B with L projected out behaves the same
  (4.976 / 2.485 / 0.947);
* a particle-hole unitary exists — W = X(x)X(x)X gives
  W f_k W^dag = (f_1^dag, −f_2^dag, f_3^dag) exactly (residual 0) — but it
  inverts the charge grading (W N W^dag = 3 − N, residual 0) and swaps
  F <-> F^dag (the image of f_1 is 100.0% orthogonal to F), so it lies outside
  the span-preserving group; SU(2), being connected, cannot live in a
  disconnected coset;
* antilinear maps add nothing new: all f_k are real matrices, so every
  antiunitary preserving F is entrywise conjugation K composed with a
  span-preserving unitary, and K G(U) K = G(conj U) (residual 0).

**Sentences Paper 2 can use (Sec. 6.5 upgrade, removing the
"structural argument, not a computation" caveat):**

> The claim "the mode gauge group is exactly U(3)" is now a machine-checked
> lemma rather than a representation-theoretic argument. The real Lie algebra
> of Hermitian h with [h, f_k] in span{f_1, f_2, f_3} has dimension exactly
> 10 = dim u(3) + 1; it equals dGamma(u(3)) (+) R·1, and each element
> exponentiates to e^{i phi} G(U) with U in U(3) (residuals < 3e-15). The
> converse holds at group level with no extra components: the CAR force the

> full matrix algebra M_8(C) (machine-checked), Schur's lemma fixes the phase.
> Within this group, the generators commuting with the colour action span only
> {1, N} — a two-dimensional abelian algebra — so no SU(2) commuting with
> SU(3)_c acts on the ideal. Allowing number-violating Bogoliubov maps
> f_i -> Sum_j (U_ij f_j + V_ij f_j^dag) does enlarge the generator space, to
> dim 16 = dim so(6) + 1, but every added direction violates the
> Hamming-weight (electric-charge) grading and fails to map the mode span into
> itself: the number-conserving part collapses back to u(3) (+) R·1 exactly,
> and the particle-hole component (e.g. X(x)X(x)X, which implements
> f_k -> (f_1^dag, −f_2^dag, f_3^dag)) inverts the grading N -> 3 − N.
> (`direction_A/strengthen.py`, results in `strengthen_results.json`.)
