# Direction B — Annotated Sources

Abstracts pulled from the arXiv API; full texts of the starred (*) papers were downloaded (ar5iv HTML) and grepped for exact statements. One-line notes state the relevance to the claim under test.

## Core: Newton's constant renormalization = entanglement entropy
- **hep-th/9401070** — Susskind, Uglum, *Black Hole Entropy in Canonical Quantum Gravity and Superstring Theory* (PRD 50, 2700). The origin claim: scalar EE/area divergence ≡ one-loop correction to gravitational coupling; open-strings-on-horizon conjecture.
- **hep-th/9412020** — Fursaev, Solodukhin, *On one-loop renormalization of black-hole entropy*. Proof that entropy divergences are removed by renormalizing G_N **and** the R² couplings (generic static BH).
- **gr-qc/9503003** — Demers, Lafrance, Myers, *Black Hole Entropy without Brick Walls*. Pauli–Villars confirmation of the Susskind–Uglum suggestion (Reissner–Nordström).
- **hep-th/9506066** — Larsen, Wilczek, *Renormalization of Black Hole Entropy and of the Gravitational Coupling Constant*. Clarifies the "precise meaning" of the S-U proportionality.
- **gr-qc/9404039** — Jacobson, *Black Hole Entropy and Induced Gravity*. Species problem resolved iff 1/G_N is entirely induced (Sakharov); template for any "coupling = entanglement" claim.
- **gr-qc/9512047** — Barvinsky, Solodukhin, *Non-minimal coupling, boundary terms and renormalization of the Einstein-Hilbert action*. The ξRφ² caveat: conical vs statistical entropy split.
- **1302.1878** — Cooperman, Luty, *Renormalization of Entanglement Entropy and the Gravitational Effective Action*. Modern clean theorem: EE made finite by exactly the gravitational counterterms; renormalized Bekenstein–Hawking formula "in accordance with a proposal of Susskind and Uglum".
- **1104.3712** (*) — Solodukhin, *Entanglement entropy of black holes* (Living Reviews). Canonical review; §8 species puzzle, nonminimal/gauge/graviton caveats; D_s(d) tables (gauge: (d−2)·dim G).
- **1502.03758** — Solodukhin, *Newton constant, contact terms and entropy*. Contact terms originate from interaction mediators (vectors, gravitons); graviton puzzle via orbifold vs n-fold cover.

## Core: Kabat contact term
- **hep-th/9503016** (*) — Kabat, *Black Hole Entropy and Entropy of Entanglement* (NPB 453, 281). THE contact-term paper. Exact: βF_vec = (d−2)βF_scalar + A_⊥(2π−β)∫ds/(4πs)^{d/2}; c₁(gauge) = (d−2)/6 − 1; negative spin-1 entropy in d=4; "contact interaction cannot be interpreted as entropy of entanglement"; field-theory limit of S-U stranded open strings.
- **hep-th/9703088** — Iellici, Moretti, *Kabat's surface terms in the zeta function approach*. Early regulator-dependence scrutiny.
- **1209.2677** — Solodukhin, *Remarks on effective action and entanglement entropy of Maxwell field in generic gauge*. Gauge-fixing-parameter independence (with matched regulators); the term is not a gauge artifact.
- **1206.5831** — Donnelly, Wall, *Do gauge fields really contribute negatively to black hole entropy?* (PRD 86, 064042). Pre-edge-mode position: contact term from zero-mode mistreatment / gauge-dependent Wald ambiguity; 2d reduced phase space gives positive entropy = EE.
- **1810.11912** — Witten, *Open Strings On The Rindler Horizon*. Modern string-theoretic take on the S-U/Kabat open-string sector (tachyon disappears after continuation in replica number).

## Core: Donnelly–Wall edge modes
- **1412.1895** (*) — Donnelly, Wall, *Entanglement entropy of electromagnetic edge modes* (PRL 114, 111603). Contact term = EE of edge modes (E_⊥-determined classical solutions); S_anom ~ −31/45 ln r, Dowker −16/45, edge −1/3; **grep verified: "beta function" appears 0 times**; renormalization link is to the *gravitational* action.
- **1506.05792** (*) — Donnelly, Wall, *Geometric entropy and edge modes of the electromagnetic field* (PRD 94, 104053). KK-reduction proof; contact term = one negative surface scalar; flux sectors (coupling-dependent, finite); "beta function" 0 times.
- **1406.7304** — Donnelly, *Entanglement entropy and nonabelian gauge symmetry* (CQG 31, 214003). Lattice definition for nonabelian G; 2d YM test (edge entropy = function of g²A); surface-charge edge dof.
- **1109.0036** — Donnelly, *Decomposition of entanglement entropy in lattice gauge theory*. Three-term decomposition: Shannon(reps) + Σp_R ln dim R + quantum.
- **1601.04744** — Donnelly, Freidel, *Local subsystems in gauge theory and gravity*. Corner-symmetry program: boundary dof for YM = boundary gauge choice; no coupling-renormalization claim.
- **1912.06025** — Geiller, Jai-akson, *Extended actions, dynamics of edge modes, and entanglement entropy*. Systematic boundary-action framework for edge modes.
- **2006.12527** — Freidel, Geiller, Pranzetti, *Edge modes of gravity I*. Gravity corner-symmetry series (context only).
- **1801.09910 / 1804.07585** — Blommaert, Mertens, Verschelde (+Zakharov), *Edge State Quantization* / *Edge Dynamics from the Path Integral*. Canonical/path-integral edge sectors for Maxwell & YM; 2d YM edge = particle-on-a-group.
- **2310.14690** — Mukherjee, *Entanglement entropy and the boundary action of edge modes*. p-form generalization of the edge log term.

## Core: algebraic/center approach and distillability
- **1312.1183** — Casini, Huerta, Rosabal, *Remarks on entanglement entropy for gauge fields* (PRD 89, 085012). Center → S = H({p_k}) + Σp_k S(ρ_k); trivial-center choices exist; ambiguities boundary-local; MI/relative entropy universal.
- **1501.02593** — Ghosh, Soni, Trivedi, *On The Entanglement Entropy For Gauge Theories*. Extended Hilbert space definition; = electric-center CHR for Z_N, U(1); matches replica trick; not distillation-operational.
- **1608.00353** — Soni, Trivedi, *Entanglement Entropy in (3+1)-d Free U(1) Gauge Theory*. Extended-HS log term = a-anomaly (−31/45); extractable part differs by "a massless scalar living on the boundary" (→ −16/45).
- **1811.06986** — Moitra, Soni, Trivedi, *Entanglement Entropy, Relative Entropy and Duality*. Classical/edge term carries cutoff-scale modes; drops out of relative entropy and MI in the continuum.
- **1808.05939** — Lin, Radičević, *Comments on Defining Entanglement Entropy*. Full vs distillable vs gauge-invariant entropies; holography computes the "full" one (conjecture).
- **0806.3376** — Buividovich, Polikarpov, *Entanglement entropy in gauge theories and the holographic principle for electric strings*. First lattice demonstration: string-endpoint (edge) entropy saturates EE in 3d Z₂.
- **1509.08478** — Radičević, *Entanglement in Weakly Coupled Lattice Gauge Theories*. 2+1d universal term (1/2)dim(G) log(e²r): coupling inside the universal EE term.
- **1512.01334** — Itou, Nagata, Nakagawa, Nakamura, Zakharov, *Entanglement in Four-Dimensional SU(3) Gauge Theory*. Lattice entropic c-function for SU(3): state of the art for nonabelian d=4.

## Core: Maxwell log coefficient / anomaly dispute
- **1009.3854** — Dowker, *Entanglement entropy for even spheres*. The −16/45 thermal computation that exposed the mismatch with −31/45.
- **1308.4964** — Eling, Oz, Theisen, *Entanglement and Thermal Entropy of Gauge Fields*. Alternative resolution via modified ⟨T_μν⟩ on conformally flat spaces.
- **1412.2730** — Huang, *Central Charge and Entangled Gauge Fields*. Alternative resolution via BRST boundary (Gibbons–Hawking-like) term.
- **1512.06182** — Casini, Huerta, *Entanglement entropy of a Maxwell field on the sphere*. Algebraic computation: free Maxwell = 2 scalars minus l=0 modes ⇒ −16/45; does **not** match anomaly.
- **1911.00529** (*) — Casini, Huerta, Magán, Pontello, *Logarithmic coefficient of the entanglement entropy of a Maxwell field* (PRD 101, 065020). The contestation: free field −16/45 is physical (mutual information); anomaly restored only with heavy electric AND magnetic charges; correction −1/3 universal, UV-dynamics independent; center/edge contributions "too singular", regularization-dependent; free-field zero-mode gives +2/3 not −1/3.
- **1908.01800** — Benedetti, Casini, *Entanglement entropy of linearized gravitons in a sphere*. Graviton log coefficient −61/45 via MI; graviton case unsettled (relevant to S-U caveat list).
- **1601.06910** — Zuo, *A note on electromagnetic edge modes*. βγ/bc CFT reading of the surface scalar; another reconciliation attempt (shows the attribution was not settled).

## Novelty-scan adjacents (confirmed distinct from the proposed conjecture)
- **1505.04753** — Jacobson, *Entanglement Equilibrium and the Einstein Equation*. The gravitational "coupling-from-entanglement" statement; no published YM analog found.
- **1812.03138** — Beane, Kaplan, Klco, Savage, *Entanglement Suppression and Emergent Symmetries of Strong Interactions*. S-matrix entanglement constrains couplings/symmetries — different object (scattering, not vacuum edge modes).
- **2605.17434** — Li, Ma, Shu, Zhu, *Entanglement Maximization and Symmetry Selection in Composite Higgs Models* (2026). Recent BSM entanglement-extremization line; not vacuum-EE couplings.
- **2209.03979** — *Emergent classical gauge symmetry from quantum entanglement*. Emergence of gauge kinematics, not coupling values.
- **1202.5650** — Casini, Huerta, *On the RG running of the entanglement entropy of a circle*. EE–RG monotonicity (c/F-theorems): the established sense in which "running" and EE are linked — anomalies/free energy, not 1/g².
